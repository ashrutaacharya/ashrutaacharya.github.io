Economics PhD Candidate | George Mason University

Ashruta Acharya

I use applied econometrics and geospatial analysis to study how infrastructure and public policy shape markets and economic opportunity.

My research combines original dataset construction and historical panel data to examine market access, structural change, and regional inequality, with a focus on South Asia.

I am interested in economist roles where empirical research informs policy and business decisions.
