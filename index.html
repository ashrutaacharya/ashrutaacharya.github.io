Ashruta Acharya

I study comparative economic history, political economy, and development, with a focus on Nepal, India, and South Asia.

My research examines how infrastructure, state capacity, institutions, and policy shocks shape long-run structural transformation, market integration, and spatial inequality. I combine archival and historical inquiry with applied econometrics and geospatial analysis.
